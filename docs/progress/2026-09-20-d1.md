# D1 handover — the Margin design system

**Date:** 2026-09-20 · **Branch:** `feat/d1-design-system` (off `main` at `98bb8fd`; not pushed) ·
**Status:** done and verified, awaiting the owner's review and pull request

Implements ADR-0013 (the Margin visual identity) across the whole app. The reference mockups stay
on `design/explorations` (`0b976e2`), which is **not** merged; only the tokens, the fonts, their
licences and `trim-fonts.sh` came across.

## What was built

**Phase 0 — baseline.** 80 screenshots of every screen before the change, and a re-measured Slow 4G
baseline. That re-measurement found the M1 handover's page weights were taken one commit before the
M1 review's web fixes landed and never redone: at `98bb8fd` the landing page was **169 KB**, not
145 KB, and sign-up **202 KB**, not 188 KB. Reproduced by rebuilding both commits in throwaway
worktrees. The capture script became `apps/web/e2e/visual/capture.spec.ts`, so later UI work gets
the same before/after for free.

**Phase 1 — tokens, fonts, primitives (`61d6853`).** `packages/ui/src/tokens.css` holds the whole
palette in both themes, and a Vitest reads that file to check every pair the ADR commits to — the
theme cannot drift from its own contrast table. The three Latin faces are trimmed to the characters
we use (55.8 KB of a 60 KB budget, enforced by a test) and rebuilt by `tools/trim-fonts.sh`. Button
gained `size="lg"`, `--primary-hover` replaced an alpha of `--primary`, focus became one 2px `--ring`
outline set in `globals.css`, and field borders moved to `--input`.

**Phase 2 — the chrome (`9c7c6bb`).** The wordmark (a dotless ı with an inline-SVG pen tick, in a
`nav` and a `paper` tone), the signed-in header on the structural grey bar, `NavLink` with
`aria-current` and the pen underline, `Margined`/`Note`/`Highlight`, and the "Readi by DegRon"
footer line.

**Phase 3 — every screen (`93e4a81`).** All 20 screens restyled, plus the landing page's Margin
hero: an annotated example answer that shows what Readi does before any heading explains it. Auth
pages moved under the same grey bar with the form in a reading column and notes in the margin;
onboarding steps are named and underlined rather than three anonymous bars; the profile's boxes
became ruled sections. A type sweep went with it: secondary text is `text-base` (14px against a 17px
body was too small), `font-medium` became `font-bold` (the sans ships 400 and 700 only), and
headings dropped `tracking-tight`, which fought the serif.

**Phase 4 — verification and docs (this commit).** The honest "being built in the open" line in the
hero, the final 80 screenshots, the measurements below, README and CLAUDE.md, and this handover.

## Deviations from ADR-0013, and why

The ADR is accepted and was not edited; these are recorded here instead (CLAUDE.md §7.4).

1. **Both sans weights are preloaded**, not only 400. `next/font` preloads per call, and 400 and 700
   must share one call to stay one family, so preloading 400 alone is not expressible. 700 sets every
   label and button, so it is fetched on first paint anyway. Owner decision, 2026-09-20.
2. **The ₦ family comes first in each stack**, where the ADR implies it sits after the main face.
   `next/font` appends its own metric-adjusted Arial fallback inside the family variable, and that
   fallback carries no `unicode-range`, so a ₦ family behind it would never be reached and ₦ would
   quietly render in Arial.
3. **27 contrast pairs, not 26.** The ADR set one page-wide focus ring and never asked what it looks
   like on the navigation bar: `#C2410C` is **1.46:1** on that grey. Inside `data-nav-surface`,
   `--ring` now resolves to `--nav-accent` (3.34:1), and the new pair — "Focus ring on the navigation
   bar" — keeps it that way. `pnpm --filter @readi/ui contrast` prints the full table.
4. **The phone tab bar and the SVG trend chart are not built.** No screen today has three
   destinations or a chart. They move to M3, where the dashboard needs them. Owner decision.
5. **`app/global-error.tsx` uses no tokens, classes or fonts.** It renders its own document outside
   the root layout, so it carries inline CSS with the palette written out in both themes; if the
   tokens or fonts are what broke, it still renders.

## ⚠ Before the landing page goes public

Every item below is live on `/` today and must be settled first.

1. **The copy is a draft.** `docs/progress/2026-09-20-d1-landing-copy.md` holds the whole text, the
   spec line behind each claim, and the keys to edit (`landing.*` in
   `apps/web/src/i18n/messages/en.json`). Nothing on that page has been signed off.
2. **Six sentences describe features that do not exist yet** — the AI interviewer and follow-ups,
   voice mode, reports and lessons, the study plan, the 15-minute diagnostic, and "3 text mock
   interviews every month". They are covered for now by the line in the hero ("Readi is being built
   in the open…"), which sits above the buttons on purpose. **When M3–M5 land, remove that line**
   — and until they do, do not delete it.
3. **No prices are shown, and none may be hardcoded.** Prices are configurable in admin (spec §4.6).
   The page says "priced in naira, weekly or monthly" and nothing more; adding a figure means
   reading it from the plan/price records, not typing it into the copy.
4. **The support email must be real.** `SUPPORT_EMAIL` (apps/api) and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL`
   (apps/web) are still `support@readi.invalid` in local config; both refuse `.invalid` in
   production, so this fails loudly rather than silently — but it is on the critical path.
5. **The `/status` link in the footer comes out before launch** (M0 follow-up N4). It reveals
   infrastructure health to anyone who reads the page.
6. **Legal pages do not exist.** The ADR puts "Readi by DegRon" on legal and billing pages, and the
   footer has no Privacy, Terms or Refunds links because there is nothing to link to. A privacy
   notice is already queued for M8, when checkout makes an email address mandatory.
7. **The example answer is an illustration**, captioned "Example feedback from a frontend mock
   interview". If it ever gets replaced by a real session, that needs the candidate's consent.
8. **PWA icons and a Lighthouse pass are still M10.**
